I am learning keyboard slowly, which is probably the right way to learn it.

It is one of the few things in my life that I do not want to optimize too aggressively. Progress is uneven, mistakes are obvious, and that is part of the appeal.

There is something useful about working on a skill that does not care about dashboards, productivity language, or short-term output.
