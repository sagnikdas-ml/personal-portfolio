Some of my favorite parts of Nürnberg are not the headline landmarks but the routes between places.

Cycling or walking through the city is a good way to step out of problem-solving mode for a while. It clears the head without feeling like a formal break.

I like keeping small visual records of those routes. They help memory stay attached to place.
