Living in Germany has changed the rhythm of my days more than I expected. Some of it is practical: public transport, university routines, paperwork, and the slow but steady work of learning the language.

Some of it is quieter. I walk more. I notice neighborhoods more. I have become more aware of the difference between being busy and paying attention.

That slower pace has been useful for study as well. Research usually benefits from patience more than urgency.
