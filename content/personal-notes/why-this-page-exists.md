Not everything belongs on a CV. Some things are better kept as thoughts: places, habits, experiments, and moments that explain the person behind the work.

I wanted this site to feel like a long-term archive, not just a hiring document. The professional side matters, but so do the quieter details that shape how I think and work.

That is what the personal page is for. It gives the site a second register: less polished, more observational, and easier to keep alive over time.
