Most photos are easy to take and easy to forget.

I like the idea of keeping a lightweight archive where images have titles, dates, and a little context. It makes them feel less disposable and more like part of a lived record.

The goal is not perfect documentation. It is just enough structure to make a memory easier to revisit later.
